Support

Stories of Jesus is a collection of cartoon games based on stories of Jesus and the parables He told, from the Gospels. It is made for children and families.

Common questions

There's no sound. Check the 🔊 button in the top corner of the game isn't muted, and that your device's volume is up. On iPhone, also check the silent switch on the side.

How do I pause or restart a level? Tap the ⏸ button. The pause menu has Resume, Restart Level and All games.

How do I change the Bible version? On the main menu, pick KJV, NKJV, NIV or NLT under the title. Every game uses the version you choose.

Does it need the internet? No. Everything is built into the app.

Does it collect any information? No. See our privacy policy.
