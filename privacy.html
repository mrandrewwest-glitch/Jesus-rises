Privacy Policy

Stories of Jesus · Last updated 12 November 2026

Stories of Jesus collects no personal information at all.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your best score in each game and which Bible version you picked. These are saved only on your own device, are never sent anywhere, and are deleted if you delete the app.

Internet

Every game, picture, sound and font is built into the app, so it works without an internet connection and does not contact any server while you play.

Children

The app is made for children and families. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children. It contains no links out of the app and no ways to buy anything.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
