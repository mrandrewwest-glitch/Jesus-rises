## Description [4000]

Stories of Jesus is a collection of 37 colourful cartoon games based on the life of Jesus and the parables He told, made for children and families.

Each game is short, simple to pick up and gets harder as you go. Every game ends with the Bible verse it comes from, so children learn the story as they play.

THE LIFE OF JESUS
Follow Jesus from the manger to the empty tomb:
• Watch the sheep by night until the angels sing
• Guide the wise men across the desert by starlight
• Search the busy streets of Jerusalem for the boy Jesus
• Lower your friend through the roof, gently and level
• Ride the donkey into Jerusalem in an endless Palm Sunday run
• Keep the boat afloat through the storm, feed the 5,000, walk on water and more

THE PARABLES
Play the stories Jesus told:
• Build your house on the rock and watch it stand through the storm
• Find the lost sheep, the lost coin and the hidden treasure
• Sow the seed, grow the mustard seed and keep your lamp burning
• Help the Good Samaritan, welcome the Prodigal Son home and more

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Choose your Bible version: KJV, NKJV, NIV or NLT
• Gentle harp and shepherd's-pipe music, with well-loved hymn tunes
• Simple tap and swipe controls for little fingers
• Pause and restart any level
• Best scores saved for every game
• Works offline: no internet needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collected

## Age rating questionnaire

Answer **None** to every content question (violence, fear, mature themes, gambling and so on), **No** to unrestricted web access and **No** to user-generated content. That gives a **4+** rating.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Choose **Data Not Collected**. The app has no analytics, ads, accounts or network calls; best scores and the Bible-version choice stay on the device.

## Notes for the App Review team

Stories of Jesus is a family game app of 37 cartoon mini-games based on Gospel stories. All games are bundled in the app and run offline. There is no login, no purchases, no ads and no data collection. To try it: pick any game on the main menu and tap Begin. Bible quotations are credited to their publishers on each game's title and results cards.
